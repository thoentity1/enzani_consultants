Who We Are

At Enzani Consultants, we believe that exceptional organisations are built by exceptional people. We are a South African learning and development consultancy dedicated to unlocking human potential, strengthening leadership capability, and enabling sustainable organisational performance.

Our approach combines strategic thinking with practical solutions, partnering with organisations to develop leaders, build high-performing teams, and create workplaces where people and businesses thrive. Every programme we design is tailored to our clients’ unique needs, ensuring measurable impact and lasting results.

The business is led by Genoulda Bestenbier, Chief Executive, an accomplished Human Capital executive with more than two decades of business experience and extensive expertise in leadership development, talent management, organisational effectiveness, succession planning, employee engagement, and performance management. Genoulda has advised executive leadership teams and Boards across complex organisations, bringing a strategic perspective that connects people strategy with business outcomes. Her passion is helping organisations develop leaders who inspire performance, foster innovation, and create cultures where people flourish.

Working alongside Genoulda is Wendy Makhubu, a Business Consultant who brings valuable consulting expertise and a collaborative approach to solving business challenges. Wendy partners with clients to understand their organisational needs and contributes to the design and delivery of practical solutions that improve capability, strengthen performance, and support sustainable growth.

Together, we bring a combination of executive leadership experience, business consulting expertise, and a deep commitment to learning and development. We believe that investing in people is the most powerful investment an organisation can make.

Whether developing future leaders, strengthening management capability, facilitating organisational change, or delivering impactful learning experiences, Enzani Consultants partners with organisations to transform potential into performance.
