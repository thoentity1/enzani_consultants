Who We Are

Enzani Consultants is a proudly South African training and human capital consulting company dedicated to empowering individuals, organisations and communities through innovative learning and development solutions.

We partner with public and private sector organisations to deliver accredited training, leadership development, executive coaching, bursary administration, learnership management and SETA support services that contribute to sustainable organisational growth.

Our client-centred approach ensures that every programme is tailored to meet business objectives while developing the knowledge, skills and confidence of employees and future leaders.

Our Story

Building people. Strengthening organisations. Creating sustainable success.

Enzani Consultants was established with a vision of helping organisations unlock the full potential of their people through professional development, strategic consulting and quality learning interventions.

Our experienced consultants understand the changing landscape of workforce development and provide practical solutions that align with organisational goals, industry requirements and national skills development priorities.

Today, we continue to support businesses, government departments and institutions by delivering high-quality learning programmes, compliance support and executive development that create lasting impact.

Genoulda Bestenbier

Chief Executive Officer/ Enzani Consultant

Genoulda Bestenbier is a seasoned Human Capital executive, leadership strategist, and learning and development specialist with more than 24 years of professional experience, including over 12 years in strategic Human Capital leadership. Throughout her career, she has partnered with executive leadership teams and Boards to develop people strategies that drive organisational performance, strengthen leadership capability, and build high-performing workplace cultures.

Genoulda holds a Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Human Resources Management from the University of Johannesburg, an MBA from Milpark Business School, and has further strengthened her executive leadership capability through the Middle Management Development Programme at GIBS and the Executive Management Programme at the Wits Business School.

Her areas of expertise include: * Leadership Development * Learning and Development Strategy * Human Capital Strategy * Executive and Management Coaching * Organisational Development * Talent Management and Succession Planning * Performance Management * Employee Engagement and Culture * Change Management * Executive Facilitation and Training

Genoulda is passionate about equipping leaders and organisations with the skills, mindset, and strategies needed to thrive in an evolving business landscape. She is recognised for combining strategic insight with practical execution, enabling organisations to unlock the full potential of their people while delivering measurable business results. As Chief Executive of Enzani Consultants, Genoulda leads the firm’s vision of partnering with organisations to develop exceptional leaders, transform workplace capability, and create sustainable organisational success through innovative learning and development solutions.

Wendy Makhubu

Executive Director

Wendy Makhubu is a dynamic business consultant with expertise in business consulting, project management, marketing, and sales. She is passionate about helping organisations improve performance through strategic planning, operational excellence, and customer-focused business solutions. With a practical and results-driven approach, Wendy partners with organisations to identify opportunities for growth, strengthen business processes, and deliver projects that create measurable value. Her ability to bridge strategy with execution enables clients to navigate change, improve efficiency, and achieve sustainable business success.

Wendy’s expertise spans a broad range of business disciplines, including: * Business Consulting * Strategic Business Planning * Project Management * Marketing Strategy * Sales Strategy and Enablement * Business Development * Stakeholder Engagement * Client Relationship Management * Process Improvement * Organisational Growth

As a Business Consultant at Enzani Consultants, Wendy works collaboratively with clients to develop practical, innovative, and sustainable solutions that support organisational growth. She is committed to building strong client partnerships and delivering consulting services that enhance business capability, improve market competitiveness, and drive long-term success. Working alongside the Enzani Consultants team, Wendy contributes to the firm’s mission of empowering organisations through strategic consulting, learning and development, and leadership excellence.
