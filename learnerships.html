Learnerships & Internships

Building Skills • Creating Opportunities • Empowering Futures

Developing Tomorrow's Workforce

Enzani Consultants partners with employers, SETAs and educational institutions to design, coordinate and manage learnership and internship programmes that equip participants with practical workplace experience while supporting organisational skills development objectives.

SETA Learnerships

Accredited learnership programmes combining structured learning with practical workplace experience, helping organisations build a skilled and compliant workforce.

Internship Programmes

Structured internship opportunities that provide graduates and unemployed youth with valuable workplace exposure while assisting employers to develop future talent.

Benefits for Employers

  • ✔ Improve workforce skills and productivity
  • ✔ Access available SETA grants
  • ✔ Support BBBEE Skills Development initiatives
  • ✔ Develop future employees
  • ✔ Enhance organisational compliance
  • ✔ Reduce recruitment costs

Benefits for Learners

  • ✔ Practical workplace experience
  • ✔ Nationally recognised qualifications
  • ✔ Career development opportunities
  • ✔ Increased employability
  • ✔ Professional mentoring and support
  • ✔ Valuable industry exposure
