SETA Support Services

Helping Organisations Maximise Skills Development and Compliance

Professional SETA Support

Enzani Consultants provides comprehensive SETA support services to help organisations effectively manage their workplace skills development obligations while maximising available grant opportunities.

Our experienced consultants work with organisations across multiple sectors to ensure compliance with Skills Development legislation and SETA requirements.

Our SETA Services

Workplace Skills Plans (WSP)

Preparation and submission of Workplace Skills Plans in accordance with SETA requirements.

Annual Training Reports (ATR)

Compilation and submission of Annual Training Reports to ensure compliance.

Grant Applications

Assistance with mandatory and discretionary grant applications and supporting documentation.

Skills Audits

Identify organisational skills gaps and align training with strategic objectives.

Learnership Administration

Planning, implementation and management of SETA learnership programmes.

Compliance Consulting

Guidance on Skills Development compliance and continuous improvement.

Benefits of Our SETA Support

  • ✔ Improve Skills Development compliance
  • ✔ Maximise available SETA grants
  • ✔ Reduce administrative burden
  • ✔ Professional reporting and documentation
  • ✔ Enhance workforce capability
  • ✔ Support BBBEE Skills Development initiatives

Our SETA Support Process
